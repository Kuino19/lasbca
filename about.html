Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles that drive our operations

Our Mission

To ensure that buildings in Lagos State are designed, constructed, and maintained to the highest standards of safety and quality through effective regulation, monitoring, and enforcement of building control laws.

We are committed to preventing building collapses, ensuring structural integrity, and promoting sustainable development practices across Lagos State.

Our Vision

To be a world-class building control agency that ensures all buildings in Lagos State meet international standards of safety, sustainability, and functionality.

We envision a Lagos where every building is safe, structurally sound, and contributes positively to the urban landscape and quality of life for all residents.

Our History

The journey of LASBCA through the years

Establishment & Growth

The Lagos State Building Control Agency (LASBCA) was established in 2010 as part of the Lagos State Government's efforts to ensure that buildings in the state meet required standards and specifications.

The agency was created in response to the increasing incidents of building collapses in the state, which had resulted in loss of lives and property. LASBCA was tasked with the responsibility of enforcing building control regulations and ensuring that all buildings in Lagos State are designed, constructed, and maintained to high standards of safety and quality.

Since its establishment, LASBCA has grown to become a vital agency in Lagos State's urban development framework, working diligently to prevent building collapses, ensure the structural integrity of buildings, and promote sustainable development practices across the state.

2010

Establishment

LASBCA was established by the Lagos State Government to enforce building control regulations and ensure compliance with building codes.

2012

Expansion of Operations

LASBCA expanded its operations to cover all local government areas in Lagos State, establishing offices in key locations.

2015

Digital Transformation

Implementation of digital systems for building plan approval and inspection processes, improving efficiency and transparency.

2018

Public Awareness Campaigns

Launch of major public awareness campaigns to educate Lagos residents about building safety and the importance of compliance with building regulations.

2020

10th Anniversary

LASBCA celebrated its 10th anniversary with a renewed commitment to ensuring building safety and quality across Lagos State.

2023

Advanced Technology Integration

Integration of advanced technologies such as drones and AI for building inspections and monitoring, enhancing the agency's capabilities.
