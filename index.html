About LASBCA

The Lagos State Building Control Agency (LASBCA) was established to enforce building control regulations and standards in Lagos State, Nigeria. Our mission is to ensure that all buildings in Lagos State are designed, constructed, and maintained to high standards of safety and quality.

We work diligently to prevent building collapses, ensure the structural integrity of buildings, and promote sustainable development practices across the state.
