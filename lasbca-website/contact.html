Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about contacting LASBCA

How long does it take to get a response to my inquiry?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours during working days. Complex inquiries may take longer to resolve, but we will keep you updated on the progress.

How can I report an illegal construction?

You can report illegal construction by calling our hotline, sending an email to complaints@lasbca.lagosstate.gov.ng, or filling out the contact form on this page. Please provide as much detail as possible, including the location and nature of the violation.

Do I need an appointment to visit a LASBCA office?

While walk-ins are accepted, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure that the appropriate staff member is available to assist you. You can schedule an appointment by calling our office or using the contact form on this page.

How can I check the status of my building plan approval application?

You can check the status of your application by visiting our headquarters or district office, calling our customer service line, or sending an email with your application reference number to info@lasbca.lagosstate.gov.ng.
