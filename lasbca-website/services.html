Our Services

Comprehensive building control services to ensure safety and compliance across Lagos State

Building Plan Approval

LASBCA reviews and approves building plans to ensure they comply with Lagos State building regulations, codes, and standards before construction begins. Our thorough review process helps prevent structural issues and ensures that buildings are designed to be safe and sustainable.

Our team of qualified professionals examines every aspect of your building plan, including structural design, electrical systems, plumbing, ventilation, accessibility features, and more to ensure compliance with all applicable regulations.

How to Apply for Building Plan Approval:

1

Submit Application

Complete and submit the building plan approval application form along with required documentation.

2

Document Review

LASBCA reviews your submitted documents for completeness and compliance with regulations.

3

Site Inspection

Our officers conduct a site inspection to verify site conditions and suitability.

4

Technical Review

Your building plans undergo thorough technical review by our team of experts.

5

Approval Issuance

Upon successful review, you will receive your building plan approval certificate.

Construction Inspection

LASBCA conducts regular inspections during the construction process to ensure that buildings are being constructed according to approved plans and in compliance with building codes and standards. Our inspections help identify and address issues early, preventing costly corrections later.

Our experienced inspectors verify structural elements, materials quality, workmanship, and safety measures at various stages of construction to ensure that your building meets all required standards.

Key Inspection Stages:

1

Foundation Inspection

Conducted before concrete is poured to verify proper excavation, reinforcement, and formwork.

2

Structural Frame Inspection

Verification of structural elements including columns, beams, and slabs.

3

Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing Inspection

Review of MEP installations before walls and ceilings are closed.

4

Final Inspection

Comprehensive inspection of the completed building before occupancy.

Building Certification

LASBCA issues certificates of completion and fitness for habitation to buildings that have successfully passed all required inspections and comply with building regulations. These certificates are essential documents that confirm your building is safe for occupancy.

Our certification process involves a thorough final inspection of the completed building to verify that it has been constructed according to approved plans and meets all safety and quality standards.

Certification Process:

1

Application Submission

Submit application for certificate of completion and fitness for habitation.

2

Final Inspection

LASBCA conducts a comprehensive final inspection of the completed building.

3

Documentation Review

Review of all construction documentation, inspection reports, and test results.

4

Certificate Issuance

Upon successful review, LASBCA issues the certificate of completion and fitness for habitation.

Distress Building Management

LASBCA identifies, monitors, and manages buildings showing signs of structural distress to prevent building collapses and ensure public safety. Our team of experts assesses distressed buildings and recommends appropriate remedial actions.

We work closely with building owners, occupants, and other stakeholders to address structural issues promptly and effectively, prioritizing the safety of Lagos residents.

Identification

Early identification of buildings showing signs of structural distress through regular surveys and public reports.

Assessment

Thorough structural assessment by qualified engineers to determine the extent and cause of distress.

Remediation

Development of remediation plans to address structural issues and restore building safety.

Enforcement

Enforcement of necessary safety measures, including evacuation when required to protect lives.
